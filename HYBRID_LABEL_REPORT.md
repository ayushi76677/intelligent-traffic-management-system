## 3. Fundamental Ground-Truth Finding & Provenance Declaration

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Zero Human Labels Exist in this Repository.**
> A thorough search across all CSV, JSON, and XML files confirmed that **no manual human ground-truth annotations exist**.
> - Detection classes (`car`, `motorcycle`, `bus`, `truck`) originate from pre-trained YOLOv8 COCO inference.
> - Congestion states originate from vehicle count thresholds ($\le 2, 3..5, \ge 6$).
> - Risk levels originate from optical bounding box size growth and downward flow heuristics.
> - Violations originate from synthetic rule thresholds.
>
> Therefore, **every single target label in this dataset is explicitly marked:**
> ```python
> label_source = "pseudo_rule_based"
> ```
> Under no circumstances are these targets described as ground truth.

## 7. Limitations of Pseudo-Rule-Based Labels

Users and model developers must be cognizant of the inherent theoretical boundaries of pseudo-rule-based targets:

1. **Circular Learning (Rule Distillation):** A model trained on pseudo-rule-based targets learns to approximate the mathematical heuristics of the rule generator rather than unobserved ground truth. This is valuable for knowledge distillation (e.g., faster inference or spatial-temporal generalization) but cannot discover causal factors outside the rule formulas.
2. **Camera Perspective Coupling:** The approaching threat labels are derived from 2D bounding box scaling ($> 8\%$ growth) and downward screen displacement. A vehicle speeding laterally across the intersection may be high risk in real life, but is assigned `SAFE` by this camera-relative heuristic.
3. **Discrete Threshold Jumps:** Fixed cutoffs (e.g. $5.0\text{ px/s}$ for stopped status, $60$ for risk warning) produce hard boundary artifacts. A vehicle with score $59.9$ is labeled `SAFE`, while $60.1$ is labeled `WARNING`.
4. **Absence of Human Intent:** Maneuver intent (`TURNING_LEFT`, `TURNING_RIGHT`) is inferred post-hoc from angular trajectory changes rather than turn signal indicators or lane navigation plans.
